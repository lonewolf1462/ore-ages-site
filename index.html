Features

Start with a pickaxe in a stone-age village and grow into a metropolis. Farm, tame, sail, trade and own what you build.

⛏️

Resource Gathering

Mine stone, ore and wood, then craft tools and materials from the stone age to the metropolis.

🏗️

Build & Expand

Raise farms, workshops and shared buildings. Grow day by day as the sun sets and rises, and keep warm at night.

🖼️

NFT Ownership

Your rare items and assets are NFTs on Solana. Own them, trade them, take them with you (devnet testing).

💰

Crypto Payments

Trade with other players in the marketplace. Devnet only for now, no real-money purchases.

🤝

Community Driven

Form communities, share neighborhood builds and community structures, tame horses, cows, goats and hens, and sail to a mangrove island full of crabs and tropical fish.

👥

Multiplayer & Shared Play

See nearby players, visit and invite friends to houses, chat with your community, build a shared neighborhood and take work-board jobs. Online play needs a working game connection.
